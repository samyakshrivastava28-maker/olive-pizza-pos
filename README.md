High-speed, offline-first Point of Sale (POS) and thermal bill printing terminal for Olive Pizza restaurants.

---

## 🌟 Highlights

- **Dine-In, Takeaway & Delivery Billing**: Split billing, table assignments, and instant item search.
- **ESC/POS Thermal Printing**: USB, Network, and Bluetooth thermal printer integration with 58mm and 80mm roll layouts.
- **Sequential Bill Generation**: Permanent consecutive bill numbers generated authoritatively by Layerbase PostgreSQL (`canonical_bills`).
- **Hold & Recall Bills**: Multi-cart parking with quick recall.
- **Indian GST Compliance**: Item-level 5% GST computation (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%).
